## Author: Nick Barbachano

Down in a forest  
In a clearing  
With no name  

All of the leaves  
Their branches  
Appeared the same  

Ashes that burned  
In the fire  
The night before  

Beginning their journey  
Through the surface  
Back to the core  

A forest of crystal  
Shown below  

Deep in this place  
Only our hearts  
Will ever know  

Emerald jewels  
Shown in the crown  
The forest king  

A glint in his eye  
His back  
Shining green  

Singing his healing  
A message  
From the moon  

All of our hearts  
Beating to his  
Lunar tune
