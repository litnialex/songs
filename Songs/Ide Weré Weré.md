##  Traditional Yoruba

> This very old song comes out of the Yoruban & Santeria traditions in Nigeria, Cuba, & Brazil. It is about those who serve the orisha Oshun and the brass bracelets they wore that Oshun asked them to rattle. One of the ancient Yoruba myths suggests the human race would not exist if Oshun had not been sent to our world. It is said her sweet and fertile waters brought life back to humanity.

Ide weré weré nita Oshun  
Ide weré were  
Ide weré weré nita Oshun  
Ide weré weré nita ya  
  
Ocha kiniba nita Oshun  
Checké checké checké  
Nita ya  
Ide weré were
